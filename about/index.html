About Dhumketu Express

A simple, searchable view of Bangladesh's stock market.

What is Dhumketu Express?

Dhumketu Express is a personal project for exploring and presenting publicly available Dhaka Stock Exchange market data in a simple interface.

The goal is straightforward: make it easy to find a listed company, see its latest available market information, and explore its historical price data.

Market data

The site currently focuses on DSE-listed stocks, including ticker symbols, company names, classifications, latest available prices, daily changes, trading volume, and selected historical data.

Built for simplicity

Dhumketu Express is intentionally lightweight. There is no account required, and the interface is designed around search, market overview, stock details, and the complete stock list.

Data timing

Market information on this site is not intended to represent a real-time trading feed. Prices and other fields may be delayed or reflect the latest available update from the underlying data source.

Project status Dhumketu Express is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Dhaka Stock Exchange.
