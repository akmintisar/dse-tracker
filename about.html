About Chrematics

A focused market information platform for Bangladesh's listed companies.

About the platform

Chrematics is a Bangladesh-focused market and investment information platform designed to make Dhaka Stock Exchange data easier to discover, understand, and explore.

The platform brings together company information, market statistics, price movements, historical data, market insights, and financial news in one accessible interface.

Market data

Chrematics covers DSE-listed securities with information such as ticker symbols, company names, classifications, latest available prices, daily changes, trading volume, and historical price data. Coverage and available fields may vary by security and data source.

Designed for accessibility

The platform is designed to be straightforward and accessible, with no account required for core market information. Users can search companies, review market activity, compare listed stocks, explore historical prices, and follow relevant financial news.

Data timing

Market information is provided for informational purposes and is not intended to represent a real-time trading feed. Prices and other fields may be delayed or reflect the latest available update from the underlying data source.
